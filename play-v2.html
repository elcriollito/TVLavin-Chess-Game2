    About CAISSA Chess

    CAISSA Chess is a full-featured online chess application with multiple game modes and analysis tools.

    Features

    • Human vs Engine: Play against Stockfish with adjustable difficulty (20 levels)
    • Human vs Human: Play with a friend on the same device
    • Engine vs Engine: Watch two AI engines battle it out
    • Analysis Mode: Analyze positions with engine evaluation
    • Board Editor: Set up custom positions
    • Game Library: Study games from World Champions and Grandmasters
    • Move Navigation: Review games move-by-move
