FAQ

Download and verification questions

Are CAISSA downloads free?

The tools listed as available here are free to use. This page does not include payments, subscriptions or activation requirements.

How was the portable checksum obtained?

The SHA-256 shown above was independently computed from the exact 244,203,091-byte public v0.2.0 ZIP. It is also pinned in the candidate downloads catalog.

Does the online converter upload my PGN?

No. Normal conversion streams the file to a dedicated worker in your browser and does not call the legacy server build endpoint.
