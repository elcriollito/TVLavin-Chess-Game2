Play and Game Options

Game Options belongs to Play. Use the gear button beside the Play board—or the mobile Menu action—to adjust the current game and board.

  • Engine – Choose the supported engine for the game.
  • Chess960 – Enable or disable Chess960 before starting a compatible game.
  • Board setup – Flip the board, paste a FEN, or edit the position.
  • Game actions – Start a new game, begin engine-versus-engine play, or export the current PGN.

Frequently asked questions

Change your public chess name

New accounts may start with a temporary name such as Player-XXXX.... This is your public chess name; your email stays private and is not used as that name.

  1. Open the user menu and choose Account & identity.
  2. Under Public chess identity, type the nickname you want in the Public nickname field.
  3. Click Check. If the nickname is unavailable or invalid, try another one.
  4. Click Save public identity. Your change is not public until you save it.

If you do nothing, the fallback name may remain visible. New games use your updated public identity; completed games may keep the name used when they were played.

Cómo cambiar tu nombre público de ajedrez

Las cuentas nuevas pueden empezar con un nombre temporal como Player-XXXX.... Este es tu nombre público de ajedrez; tu correo electrónico permanece privado y no se usa como ese nombre.

  1. Abre el menú de usuario y elige Account & identity.
  2. En Public chess identity, escribe el apodo que quieras en el campo Public nickname.
  3. Haz clic en Check. Si el apodo no está disponible o no es válido, prueba otro.
  4. Haz clic en Save public identity. El cambio no será público hasta que lo guardes.

Si no haces nada, el nombre temporal puede seguir visible. Las partidas nuevas usan tu identidad pública actualizada; las partidas terminadas pueden conservar el nombre con el que se jugaron.

Why does CAISSA Classic open first?

Classic is the default homepage experience. Direct workspace links such as Play, Analyze, Help, and Endgame Library preserve their requested destination on a cold load.

Where did global Settings go?

The former Settings destination contained game and board controls, so those controls now live contextually in Play under Game Options.

How do I close Game Options?

Use the X button, press Escape, or select outside the dialog. Your current game remains in place.

Where can I send feedback?
