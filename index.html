    About CAISSA Chess

    CAISSA Chess is a full-featured online chess application with multiple game modes and analysis tools.

    Features

    • Human vs Engine: Play against Stockfish with adjustable difficulty (20 levels)
    • Human vs Human: Play with a friend on the same device
    • Engine vs Engine: Watch two AI engines battle it out
    • Analysis Mode: Analyze positions with engine evaluation
    • Board Editor: Set up custom positions
    • Game Library: Study games from World Champions and Grandmasters
    • Move Navigation: Review games move-by-move

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I change engine difficulty?

    In the Play section, use "Engine Settings" panel on the right to adjust Stockfish strength from beginner to maximum.

    Can I import my Chess.com/Lichess games?

    Yes! Go to Analyze section, enter your username, and CAISSA will fetch your recent games for analysis.

    What is Caissa Insight?

    Caissa Insight analyzes your games and generates a personalized coaching report with strengths, weaknesses, and improvement tips.

    Is my data stored?

    CAISSA stores data locally in your browser. Game Library uses IndexedDB. No data is sent to external servers except when using AI features.

    How do I use Mentor AI?

    Click Mentor in the sidebar, send the current position, then ask questions about plans, moves, or concepts.
