## PGNMentor URL Patterns

Base URL: `https://www.pgnmentor.com/players/`

Pattern: `https://www.pgnmentor.com/players/[LastName].zip`

Examples:
- `https://www.pgnmentor.com/players/Kasparov.zip`
- `https://www.pgnmentor.com/players/Fischer.zip`
- `https://www.pgnmentor.com/players/Morphy.zip`

Note: Some players may use comma notation:
- `Lasker,Em.zip` (to distinguish from E. Lasker)
- `Polgar,Ju.zip` (to distinguish from sisters)
