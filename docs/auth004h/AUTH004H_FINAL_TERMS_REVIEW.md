## Legally material alternatives for counsel/owner review

1. **Disputes:** court-only terms with an identified governing law and forum, or no choice-of-law clause. Arbitration/class-waiver language should not be introduced without specific counsel advice and owner approval.
2. **Liability:** a jurisdiction-aware cap with non-waivable exceptions, or a narrow disclaimer without a monetary cap. The operator's individual status must be considered.
3. **Restrictions:** immediate action for security emergencies, defined notice where safe, and a privacy contact appeal route; or a simpler no-formal-appeal beta policy. Required record retention must be specified.
4. **Changes:** advance notice for material changes with effective date and reacceptance where necessary, or notice on next sign-in. Applicable law may constrain the choice.

Conclusion: technically consistent but **not publication-ready until the C/D items are resolved**.
