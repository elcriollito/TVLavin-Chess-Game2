## Product and truth boundary

This is a **2D-only** acquisition/generation step: digital boards, screenshots, photographed screens, 2D broadcasts, print and stylized diagrams. Physical/volumetric 3D remains outside MVP. The Phase 2C public UI and the recognition route are unchanged; **VISUAL-FREEZE exception requires Alexander's explicit approval.** No classifier was trained, integrated, merged or deployed.

The historical TFJS model's 65.93% real 13-class square accuracy, 17.09% occupied piece-type accuracy, 5.41% exact black-piece accuracy and 0/31 exact boards are in the [baseline report](CAISSA_SCANNER_HISTORICAL_CLASSIFIER_BASELINE.md). They are not a v0.2 result. The 31 human-verified boards (1,984 squares) remain **evaluation-only/test-only** with their external source pixels, corners and truth unchanged. Their piece-art families and platform IDs are unrecorded; digital/livestream category tags cannot certify a platform.

## Generation and splits

Run `npm run generate:scanner:piece-dataset -- --seed=306 '--output-dir=C:\Temp\new-unique-dataset-folder'`. The destination must be new and outside the repository and certified source/truth directories. The generator refuses overwrites. It verifies the acquisition catalog/byte hashes, external real truth and its source hashes, renders synthetic tiles, then writes a local-only manifest, tiles and quality report. A repeat at the same seed produced identical manifest SHA-256 `5CEC28972193C6E2F5FD59695B13082887227BB88E686027A345F32E71FBBA46`. No timestamp or absolute machine path is embedded. Generated pixels are not committed to Git.

Each of eight families contributes 480 independent-square tiles: ten board themes × light/dark × 12 occupied classes plus 12 empties. Total synthetic **3,840**; 1,920 empty and 1,920 occupied; 960 white and 960 black; **160 per occupied class**. Train has six families/2,880 tiles, validation one/480, synthetic test one/480. The separate 31-board real test cohort has 1,984 logical square records and **no committed source pixels**. Combined manifest has 5,824 records, but synthetic and real counts must never be pooled into a training balance or accuracy metric. Whole piece family, source, augmentation instance and platform session cannot leak across splits. Board themes and augmentation *types* deliberately occur across family splits so the held-out variable is piece artwork, not background. RhosGFX/Celtic sprites and derivatives never occur in train.

Ten board themes are green/white, blue/white, brown/beige, wood-like, grayscale, hatched print, cream/gray, dark digital, light digital and low-contrast print. Themes are not piece families. All source tiles retain 128×128 color. SVG tiles embed a pinned normalized PNG for acquired occupied pieces; JPEG/WebP variants make a bounded quality-84 lossy roundtrip before lossless PNG storage. Other variants are clean, low contrast, soft blur, print fade, last-move-style highlight, coordinate glyph, mild glare, screen moiré approximation, arrow overlap, speckle, brightness gradient, desaturated print, yellowed-paper tint, subpixel scaling and mild affine perspective residual. These are controlled synthetic approximations, **not** validated real camera, page-curvature, browser-panel, logo or broadcast distributions. No 32×32 grayscale historical bottleneck is reintroduced.

The generated hard subsets contain 1,920 same-type white/black paired occupied tiles under matching conditions, 960 B/N/Q tiles, 320 king-color tiles (both kings on both tones for every family), 240 low-contrast-augmentation tiles and 1,536 print-theme tiles. B/N/Q and king tags overlap the color-pair set. Empty squares share the themes and receive highlights, arrows, coordinates, glare, print texture and compression variants. The 31-board real cohort separately contains 215 B/N/Q, 62 king, 128 low-contrast-tagged, 576 print-category and 64 livestream-category square records; these may overlap and are not training material.

## Platform coverage and remaining gaps

The [coverage matrix](../scanner/recognition/datasets/pieces/catalog/platform-coverage-v1.json) has explicit training-representative family, platform-identified real sample, human piece truth and localization truth flags for each of nine priority ecosystems. Only **Lichess** has rights-certified training artwork (seven families). It still lacks an identified real Lichess evaluation sample. Chess.com, ChessBase/Playchess, ICC, PlayOK, FIDE/event, Chessworld, mobile/web games and broadcast boards have **no certified platform-specific training/evaluation pair**. A generic livestream-category or digital board is not assigned a platform by appearance. Open representative alternatives and user-identified, rights-governed real evaluation samples are future work. No platform-level model claim is made.
