# Season 10 Changelog

Release package version: 10.0.0. Status: prepared, not deployed.

## Added

- Board-First Simplified Play shell with Games, evaluation, lifecycle, clocks, records, PostGame, and Analyze handoff.
- Foundation-level Bots, Coach, Mentor Review, Critical Moments, Guided Replay, Knowledge mapping, and Mentor Summary.
- Production-blocked Players contracts and provider boundaries.
- Local, bounded analytics observers and versioned governance.

## Changed and improved

- Canonical QA routing, legacy compatibility, board ownership, responsive layouts, visual components, CAISSA identity, themes, Worker isolation, lazy loading, event disposal, and performance budgets.

## Security and privacy

- Exact hostile-input contracts, FairPlay boundaries, isolated Workers, no Play analytics identity/chess content/storage/cookies/network, and no Mentor Memory/Mastery writes.

## Accessibility and performance

- Keyboard/focus/announcement foundations, responsive geometry, reduced-motion behavior, resource budgets, and deterministic lifecycle cleanup. Physical-device and screen-reader certification remain pending.

## Testing

- Consolidated unit, integration, responsive, cross-browser, hard-invariant, analytics, manual-chess, and repository regression coverage.

## Gated / not activated

- Simplified Play remains QA-only; Classic and Legacy Play remain defaults.
- Players runtime, public beta, production analytics transport/consent, and uncertified external integrations are not activated.

## Known limitations

- Worker/FICS/tablebase production certification, field performance/analytics, physical devices, screen readers, and repetition/fifty-move UI characterization remain open.
