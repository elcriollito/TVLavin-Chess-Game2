# Yahoo Chess and CAISSA Classic article research

Accessed: 2026-07-24

This private editorial record distinguishes the history of Yahoo Games from
community recollection and from the public features of CAISSA Classic. The
article uses original paraphrase and does not reproduce Yahoo interface assets,
logos, slogans, or proprietary text.

## Contemporary reporting on Yahoo Games

- Source: *Yahoo Games Has Passed Away at Just 17*, WIRED, 2016-02-04.
- URL: https://www.wired.com/2016/02/yahoo-games-shutting-down/
- Supports: Yahoo Games grew from a late-1990s game portal; its classic board
  and card games were browser-based social spaces; many older versions were
  removed in 2014; Yahoo Games was marked for closure in 2016.
- Reliability: Contemporary technology reporting with first-person cultural
  context. The article's headline age was corrected, so the CAISSA article does
  not rely on an exact service age.

- Source: *Yahoo Games is shutting down in May*, Engadget, 2016-03-15.
- URL: https://www.engadget.com/2016-03-15-yahoo-games-shut-down-may.html
- Supports: Yahoo announced that the remaining Yahoo Games portal would close
  on 2016-05-13; payment acceptance stopped earlier.
- Reliability: Contemporary report of Yahoo's announced shutdown date.

- Source: *After nearly 20 years, Yahoo Games is shutting down*, Game
  Developer, 2016-03-11.
- URL: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/after-nearly-20-years-yahoo-games-is-shutting-down
- Supports: The portal dated to the late 1990s and was scheduled to close in
  May 2016; it had historical importance to browser-based casual games.
- Reliability: Established games-industry publication reporting at the time.

- Source: *Yahoo's Spring Cleaning Sweeps Away Games, Livetext, and Regional
  Sites*, Fortune, 2016-03-11.
- URL: https://fortune.com/2016/03/11/yahoo-games-livetext-shuttering/
- Supports: Yahoo's games site and publishing channel were scheduled to close
  on 2016-05-13 in several markets.
- Reliability: Contemporary business reporting.

## Primary corporate record

- Source: *Let The Yahoo! Games Begin*, archived Yahoo corporate press release.
- URL: https://www.altaba.com/static-files/ec56748f-b41e-4e97-8110-989a88aa2eb8
- Supports: Yahoo promoted free Java-based play with other internet users and
  listed Chess among its classic board games.
- Reliability: Primary historical corporate record. It supports only the
  features stated in the release; it is not used for claims about later player
  sentiment.

## Secondary chess reference

- Source: *Yahoo! Chess*, Chess.com Chess Terms.
- URL: https://www.chess.com/terms/yahoo-chess
- Supports: Yahoo Chess belonged to Yahoo Games, provided online play, and the
  classic chess offering was among services removed in 2014.
- Reliability: Established chess publication, used as secondary corroboration.

## Date and scope decision

- Sources distinguish two milestones: most classic Yahoo Games, including the
  familiar chess experience, were removed in 2014; the remaining Yahoo Games
  portal closed on 2016-05-13.
- The public article states both milestones and does not collapse them into one
  unsupported “Yahoo Chess shutdown date.”
- No causal claim is made beyond Yahoo's publicly reported product
  discontinuation. The article does not speculate about internal decisions.
