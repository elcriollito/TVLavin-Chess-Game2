# CAISSA Legal Human Decision Register

**Prepared:** 2026-10-26
This register stops at the human gate. It does not make filings, publish policies, accept contracts or change production.

## HD-001 - Registered-account age and Clerk

- **Decision:** how to reconcile provisional 13+ accounts with Clerk's under-16 verifiable-parental-consent term.
- **Why:** registration cannot safely reopen while policy and provider contract conflict.
- **Options:** (A) interim 16+; (B) 13-15 VPC program with Clerk's written acceptance; (C) alternative provider/architecture; (D) keep signup paused.
- **Consequences:** A is simplest but excludes 13-15; B/C add substantial privacy/identity/security work; D delays growth.
- **Recommended next action:** retain pause and obtain privacy/technology counsel plus Clerk written guidance; use 16+ only if owner/counsel deliberately approve.
- **Counsel:** **Required** for B/C; strongly recommended for final policy.

## HD-002 - Privacy and Terms publication

- **Decision:** approve accurate public documents and effective date.
- **Why:** current routes are 404 and Google/Clerk release depends on accurate disclosures.
- **Options:** approve current simplified drafts after review; revise material identity/retention/jurisdiction positions; delay OAuth.
- **Consequences:** publication creates representations that must match behavior.
- **Recommended next action:** counsel reviews material clauses; owner approves; AUTH-004 publishes and verifies.
- **Counsel:** **Recommended**, especially age, liability, dispute and international language.

## HD-003 - Public operator identity/address

- **Decision:** whether any postal/legal identity must be public for current pages or future filings.
- **Why:** the owner wants residential privacy; some filings (for example DMCA agent) require public contact details.
- **Options:** public name only where lawful; obtain a lawful P.O. box/service address; make a filing that necessarily discloses details; defer optional filing.
- **Consequences:** privacy, cost and eligibility differ.
- **Recommended next action:** do not publish a residential address or invent an entity; ask Florida/copyright counsel at the triggering filing.
- **Counsel:** **Required** before concluding a required disclosure can be omitted.

## HD-004 - Original source license

- **Decision:** license for CAISSA-authored code and copyright-holder identity.
- **Why:** `package.json` says MIT while root `LICENSE` is absent.
- **Options:** adopt MIT for a defined scope; choose another license; keep proprietary/all-rights-reserved portions and correct metadata.
- **Consequences:** affects contributors, redistribution, commercial reuse and notice design.
- **Recommended next action:** owner inventories authorship/third-party boundaries and obtains IP review before adding root terms.
- **Counsel:** **Recommended**.

## HD-005 - Scanner historical retention and training

- **Decision:** retention/deletion criteria for 96 historical objects and whether any future dataset may use them.
- **Why:** rights/consent/lineage are incomplete and no automated schedule is live.
- **Options:** delete/quarantine unclear items; retain narrowly for documented review; certify eligible records; permanently exclude from training.
- **Consequences:** privacy, evidence and model-improvement tradeoffs.
- **Recommended next action:** reconcile record by record; default unclear evidence to quarantine/no training; approve prospective rules before new intake.
- **Counsel:** **Recommended** for rights/consent and deletion exceptions.

## HD-006 - DMCA agent and UGC posture

- **Decision:** whether current/future hosting warrants a registered DMCA agent and repeat-infringer process.
- **Why:** safe harbor is conditional and registration publishes operator contact information.
- **Options:** register now with lawful public contact; wait until material user-hosted content; limit/disable hosting.
- **Consequences:** cost/privacy/process burden versus safe-harbor eligibility.
- **Recommended next action:** counsel maps current Scanner/PGN/community storage to section 512 before forum launch.
- **Counsel:** **Required** for final safe-harbor strategy.

## HD-007 - Florida DBA/entity timing

- **Decision:** when compensated activity should trigger DBA/entity/tax planning.
- **Why:** current free facts do not clearly establish a filing duty, but future commerce changes the analysis.
- **Options:** no current filing; prepare before monetization; voluntarily form/register earlier.
- **Consequences:** cost, public identity, liability/tax administration.
- **Recommended next action:** no filing from this audit; set a hard pre-monetization counsel gate.
- **Counsel:** **Required** at monetization trigger.

## HD-008 - Security/Privacy Season 2 release

- **Decision:** whether to integrate and deploy the existing remediation branches after independent review.
- **Why:** current `main` retains SEC-001..005 and SCAN-001/003 gaps.
- **Options:** review/merge full integration branch; selectively reimplement verified fixes; keep features constrained/disabled.
- **Consequences:** migrations/env requirements and regression risk; leaving current exposure.
- **Recommended next action:** separate technical release with migration rehearsal, protected preview, load/resource tests and rollback.
- **Counsel:** not normally required for code choice; privacy counsel should approve changed consent/retention representations.

## HD-009 - International offering

- **Decision:** whether CAISSA intentionally offers to/monitors users in EEA/UK or other regulated territories.
- **Why:** accessibility alone does not establish GDPR scope, but targeting/monitoring can.
- **Options:** global general availability without targeting; intentional regional offering with compliance program; regional restrictions where lawful/appropriate.
- **Consequences:** lawful bases, rights, transfers, controller identity, representatives and notices.
- **Recommended next action:** document user/marketing/analytics facts before making jurisdiction claims.
- **Counsel:** **Required** if intentionally targeting those regions.

## HD-010 - Future commerce and competitions

- **Decision:** whether/when to activate subscriptions, donations, prizes or paid entry.
- **Why:** current service is free and PayPal retired; activation changes identity, tax and consumer duties.
- **Options:** remain free; subscriptions; one-time support; free prizes; paid-entry/cash-prize events.
- **Consequences:** materially different legal/payment/tax gates.
- **Recommended next action:** keep disabled until a product-specific counsel and release packet passes COM-001/FUT-003.
- **Counsel:** **Required** for prize/paid-entry design; recommended for all monetization.
