# CAISSA Online and Community Policy Review

**Verified:** 2026-10-26
**Current boundary:** CAISSA Online has early production data; a broad native forum/chat/DM product was not verified and is treated as planned.

## Current findings

### COMM-001 - Proportionate rules and reporting are needed before scale

Current public identity, rating, presence, matchmaking/fair-play surfaces justify short rules covering acceptable names, harassment, impersonation, spam, cheating assistance, privacy, sanctions and appeals. Provide an accessible report channel, evidence minimization, response priorities and emergency escalation. Do not describe inactive reporting/moderation UI as operational.

### COMM-002 - Fair-play conclusions need due process

Cheater Insight and similar analytics are informational, not an enforcement verdict. Avoid public accusations or automated sanctions based solely on a score. Preserve source/date/method, restrict evidence access, provide human review and an appeal route, and distinguish CAISSA decisions from Chess.com/Lichess actions.

### COMM-003 - Third-party network/content boundaries

FICS, external streams, broadcasts and linked platform data remain subject to their providers' terms, privacy, attribution, trademarks and moderation. CAISSA should clearly identify source and avoid sponsorship claims. Do not scrape or redistribute merely because content is public.

## Minimum current policy

- Be respectful; no harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, doxxing, impersonation or spam.
- No cheating tools during rated/competitive play or manipulation of results/ratings.
- Usernames/profile text may be removed when unlawful, unsafe or deceptive.
- Reports are private; knowingly false reports may be sanctioned.
- Sanctions should be proportionate and recorded; serious sanctions receive an appeal path.
- Users must not upload/share content they lack rights to disclose.
- Safety/security reports may be escalated and retained narrowly.
- Age eligibility and child-safety rules apply independently of community rules.

## Forum/chat/DM activation gate

Before enabling broad UGC or direct communication:

1. Define public/private visibility, data retention, block/mute/report, appeals and moderator permissions.
2. Staff moderation and escalation hours proportionate to actual risk; prohibit launch with no accountable reviewer.
3. Add anti-spam/rate limits, account-abuse controls, evidence access logs and moderator security.
4. Establish child-safety design, grooming/sexual-content rules and legally reviewed emergency/CSAM reporting procedures.
5. Publish content license/ownership terms and a copyright notice/counter-notice process.
6. Decide with counsel whether to register a DMCA agent. Safe harbor is conditional, not blanket immunity; all eligible OSPs need a reasonably implemented repeat-infringer policy and standard-technical-measure accommodation.
7. Analyze section 230 and state law for the actual product. Do not state that section 230 eliminates all platform liability.
8. Complete accessibility review for composer, report dialog, moderation status and notifications.

## Tournaments

Ordinary free chess play and free/no-prize tournaments are not classified here as gambling. Prize or paid-entry events require separate rules, eligibility/age/geography, prize administration, tax, fraud and skill-contest/gambling counsel review. Never combine the four tournament models into one approval.

## Minors

Until age/provider gates are resolved, avoid public messaging, image sharing and persistent social graphs for minors. CAISSA Junior requires a separate parent-controlled community design; it is not an extension of adult forum defaults.
