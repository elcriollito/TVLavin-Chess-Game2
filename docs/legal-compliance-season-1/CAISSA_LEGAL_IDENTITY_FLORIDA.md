# CAISSA Legal Identity and Florida Review

**Verified:** 2026-10-26
**Finding:** FL-001
**Current operator facts:** individual, personal project, free access, no company, no active subscriptions and no commercial revenue.

This report does not determine legal status or replace Florida counsel.

## Present conclusion

Florida section 865.09 defines a covered "business" as an enterprise dealing in things of value or rendering services for compensation. On the verified free/no-revenue facts, a mandatory fictitious-name registration for the public name CAISSA Chess is not clearly established. No filing should be made from this audit. The answer must be revisited before compensation, paid services, sponsorship, donations, prizes, subscriptions or other commercial activity begins.

A fictitious-name registration is public notice, not trademark ownership and not a liability shield. It requires public identity/address information. A future LLC is optional organizational planning, not a current compliance requirement inferred merely from operating a free website.

## Identity and contact design

- Public service identity: **CAISSA Chess**.
- Public privacy contact: `privacy@caissa-chess.org`.
- Private forwarding destination and residential address must not appear in Git or routine public documents.
- Privacy/Terms should truthfully state that the service is operated by an individual under the CAISSA Chess name. Do not invent an LLC, registered office or business address.
- Counsel should determine whether a postal/contact address must be published for a particular filing, DMCA designation, consumer program or jurisdiction. A lawful P.O. box or registered-agent/service address may be evaluated; do not represent it as already established.

## Trigger matrix

| Trigger | Required re-review |
|---|---|
| Subscriptions, paid digital goods, paid tournaments, donations or sponsorship | DBA/entity/tax/consumer/payment review before activation |
| Hiring or contracting | IP assignment, tax, data access and confidentiality |
| DMCA agent filing | Legal operator name plus a public physical/P.O. Box address, phone and email |
| Trademark filing or app-store launch | Clearance search, ownership identity and specimens |
| Material revenue/liability exposure | Entity formation, insurance, accounting and contracts |
| International offering/targeting | Controller identity, representative and consumer-law analysis where applicable |

## Trademark and domain

Use of CAISSA Chess can create rights and conflicts independently of a fictitious-name filing. Before commercialization, merchandise or native-app distribution, commission a professional clearance search and decide ownership. Keep provider names nominative and avoid sponsorship claims. Domain ownership evidence may remain private but should be recoverable in an operator vault.

## Florida privacy statutes

- Florida Digital Bill of Rights controller status is not met on current facts because section 501.702 requires, among other conditions, more than $1 billion in global annual revenue and specified business models.
- Florida section 501.171 defines covered entity in commercial terms. Applicability to a free personal project remains a counsel question; proportionate incident response and security are still prudent.
- Section 501.1735 may apply to an online game predominantly accessed by children. CAISSA's actual audience composition is unknown; this is a child-safety applicability question, not a DBA question.

## Human decision

Do not file a DBA or form an entity merely because this audit exists. Obtain Florida counsel before monetization or a public filing that would expose private identity/address information.
