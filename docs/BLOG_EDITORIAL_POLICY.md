# CAISSA Chess public editorial policy

Only entries with `status: "published"` in `data/blog-articles.json` may be rendered, linked, included in structured data, or added to the sitemap. Drafts are source-only editorial records and must never receive a public route.

Before publication, an editor must verify that the article is original, substantial, fact-checked, and limited to public subject matter: CAISSA history, released features, public chess history, and user-level chess education. Articles must not disclose source code, repository or infrastructure details, endpoints, authentication or security internals, private configuration, proprietary implementation, unreleased specifications, detailed roadmaps, or unannounced monetization.

Future-facing copy must stay broad and make no feature or date promises. Approved example: “CAISSA continues exploring new ways to help players learn, study and enjoy chess.”

Every published entry must include all fields defined in `data/blog-article.schema.json`. The publisher and author byline is always `CAISSA Chess Editorial`. Publication review must also confirm image rights, descriptive alt text, working internal links, unique metadata, and consistency between visible content and structured data.
