# Community and Fair-Play Policy Readiness

Status: POLICY CHECKLIST READY FOR APPROVAL; SOCIAL FEATURES NOT STARTED.

## Current proportional controls

CAISSA should publish a concise conduct/fair-play notice before authenticated competitive features expand. It should distinguish assistance permitted in study/analysis from prohibited real-time assistance in rated or prize play, provide a private report channel, state that automated signals are evidence rather than conclusive guilt, and offer notice plus appeal for material sanctions except where safety/security requires temporary restriction.

## Required moderation workflow

- Categorize reports: cheating, harassment, impersonation, illegal content, IP/DMCA, privacy/safety and technical abuse.
- Minimize reports; do not solicit passwords, full tokens or unnecessary identity documents.
- Preserve source, timestamp, reporter, affected surface and access controls.
- Separate emergency access restriction from final adjudication.
- Require human review before durable cheating findings or public labels.
- Record decision, evidence, conflicts, notice, appeal, outcome and retention basis.
- Protect reporters and accused users from unnecessary disclosure.

## Future forum/DM gate

Before activation: community terms, reporting UI, blocking, rate limits, anti-spam, moderator roles, audit trail, appeals, minor-safety design, retention/deletion behavior, law-enforcement request procedure, accessibility and incident response must be implemented and tested.

## DMCA readiness

Prepare a takedown/counter-notice workflow, repeat-infringer policy and evidence register. Do not register an agent, publish a private residential address or claim safe-harbor completion without owner approval and qualified counsel.

Forums, DMs, public profiles and new social features remain NOT STARTED.
