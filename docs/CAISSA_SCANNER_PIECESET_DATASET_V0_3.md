## Distinctiveness, split and balance

Integrity checks verified **120/120 unique original SVG hashes**, **120/120 unique normalized PNG hashes**, all ten acquired-family license/notice hashes and all 12 symbols per family. There are **zero cross-family exact-byte duplicates** and **zero flagged near-duplicates** in 45 acquired-family comparisons. The structural screen compares all twelve 32×32 alpha occupancy and edge maps with mean Hamming thresholds of 0.08 each; the closest pair, Chessnut/Livius, measures occupancy 0.0996 and edge 0.1431. It is a screening tool, not semantic identity proof; the procedural family has no raster assets to compare. Same-family white/black shape pairs are reported separately and do not inflate family count. Board recolors/augmentations are never new piece families.

Whole-family split is **7 train / 2 validation / 2 test**. Train: CAISSA procedural, Chessnut, Fantasy, Spatial, Kiwen Suwi, Firi, Kosal. Validation: Celtic, Livius. Test: RhosGFX, P4wn SVG. No piece family, source image, augmentation instance or platform session crosses splits. Held-out artwork is absent from training; ten shared board themes and augmentation *types* are intentional controlled factors.

Each family supplies 480 128×128 tiles. Total synthetic: **5,280** — 2,640 empty and 2,640 occupied, 1,320 white and 1,320 black, **220 per occupied class**. Train alone: 3,360 tiles — 1,680 empty/occupied, 840 white/black, 140 per occupied class. All black `p/n/b/r/q/k` classes have symmetric support. The unchanged augmentation policy yields **2,640 same-type color-paired occupied tiles**, **1,320 bishop/knight/queen tiles**, **440 king-color tiles**, **330 low-contrast augmentation tiles** and **2,112 print-theme tiles** across the synthetic set. Both king colors occur on both square tones in every family. Hard-subset counts overlap.

## Platform evaluation governance

The matrix covers Chess.com, Lichess, ChessBase/Playchess, ICC, PlayOK, FIDE/event, Chessworld, mobile/web games and broadcast boards. Lichess has seven directly licensed training families; generic mobile/web games now have a directly licensed P4wn family. Other named platforms have **no certified direct training asset**; stylistic resemblance is not a platform claim. No platform currently has sample-ID-matched real evaluation identity, platform-specific human piece truth or platform-specific localization truth. Generic livestream/digital tags and a historical visual note about two Playchess views cannot certify a numbered file's platform identity. Every platform retains an evaluation gap; no per-platform success rate is reported.

The candidate pool binds **33 local originals and 33 Chessvision reference screenshots** by SHA-256, with **14 known aliases to the prior v0.1 corpus**. Its `platformId`, source type, reference success/failure and human-verified outcome remain null/unknown because Alexander's narrative examples lack an unambiguous numbered-file mapping. Board presence was human-verified, but a reference screenshot is not verified 64-square piece truth. All 33 are `fresh-evaluation-candidate`, not formal benchmark additions, and forbid training. Source identity, human outcome and piece truth must be linked to sample IDs before any platform score or new formal cohort. No platform identity is inferred from appearance.

Readiness is **READY FOR BASELINE TRAINING** for a later generalized *baseline experiment only*: rights/provenance, 11 eligible families, four external upstreams, 2+2 whole-family holdouts, class/color balance, no detected cross-family duplicate, deterministic generation and unchanged real benchmark meet the stated minimum. This is **not** production-recognition or platform-generalization certification.
