# CAISSA Junior future product boundary

Status: product reference only; not implemented; legal and Human Release Gates required

CAISSA Junior is not part of AUTH-004. This document preserves a future direction without authorizing code, accounts, parental outreach, or collection of children's information.

## Product reference

Lichess Kid Mode is a useful product reference because it allows chess play while sharply limiting social exposure: ordinary direct messages, forums, blogs, streams and videos are restricted, with narrowly scoped communication in supervised classes. CAISSA must not treat that product pattern as legal compliance, parental consent, contractual authorization, or a substitute for its own risk assessment.

## Proposed safeguards for future review

- Default to private, child-safe play and learning surfaces with no public profile discovery.
- Permit chess moves and system-generated learning feedback; disable open chat, direct messages, forums, public posts, livestream interaction, user-generated links, profile biographies and unrestricted uploads.
- Use age-appropriate names or aliases without publishing legal names, email addresses, school names or location.
- Require a separately designed, verifiable parental-consent workflow where applicable, with clear notice, authenticated parent controls, consent records, revocation and deletion rights.
- Give the parent or authorized teacher control of recovery and social settings; a child must not be able to disable protected mode.
- Minimize collection, prohibit behavioral advertising, keep analytics off by default, and define short child-specific retention periods before launch.
- Add abuse reporting, moderation, fair-play, safety escalation and human support procedures suitable for younger users.
- Separate teacher/class authority from general account authority and prevent one adult from silently expanding another family's consent.
- Test accessibility, comprehension, dark-pattern resistance and safe failure behavior with qualified reviewers before any child-facing pilot.

## Mandatory future gates

1. Qualified counsel approves audience classification, notice, consent method, operator obligations, contracts and supported jurisdictions.
2. Clerk confirms the proposed use is permitted and CAISSA can provide required evidence of verifiable parental consent.
3. Privacy, security, retention, deletion, incident response and data-rights designs are approved and tested.
4. Child-safe social restrictions are enforced server-side and cannot be changed by the child or bypassed through APIs.
5. A closed, supervised pilot is separately approved before any broader availability.
