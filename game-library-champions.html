The lineage

Eighteen keepers of the crown

Original portrait art and archival monograms form the archive’s visual identity. Select a champion to explore the reign, title transition, defining matches and available PGN collections.

The archive covers the recognized World Championship lineage and its major split-title era.

  1. Wilhelm Steinitz, 1886–1894
  2. Emanuel Lasker, 1894–1921
  3. José Raúl Capablanca, 1921–1927
  4. Alexander Alekhine, 1927–1935 and 1937–1946
  5. Max Euwe, 1935–1937
  6. Mikhail Botvinnik, 1948–1957, 1958–1960 and 1961–1963
  7. Vasily Smyslov, 1957–1958
  8. Mikhail Tal, 1960–1961
  9. Tigran Petrosian, 1963–1969
  10. Boris Spassky, 1969–1972
  11. Bobby Fischer, 1972–1975
  12. Anatoly Karpov, 1975–1985 and FIDE champion from 1993
  13. Garry Kasparov, 1985–2000 across the undisputed and Classical lineages
  14. Vladimir Kramnik, Classical champion from 2000 and reunification champion in 2006
  15. Viswanathan Anand, FIDE champion from 2000 and undisputed champion from 2007
  16. Magnus Carlsen, 2013–2023
  17. Ding Liren, 2023–2024
  18. Gukesh Dommaraju, 2024–present

1993–2006 · two claims, one history

The crown divided.

Kasparov and Short’s break with FIDE created concurrent Classical and FIDE title lines. The archive follows both paths until Kramnik and Topalov reunify the title in Elista.
