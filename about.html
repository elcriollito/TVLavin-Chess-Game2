About CAISSA Chess

AI-powered chess analysis for everyone

Our Mission

CAISSA Chess exists to democratize chess analysis. We believe every chess player, from beginner to master, deserves access to powerful, intuitive tools for improving their game.

Traditional chess analysis tools can be expensive, complex, or difficult to turn into practical learning. We are building an accessible platform that combines strong analysis with clear educational guidance.

What Makes CAISSA Different

Engine-Powered Analysis

Strong chess analysis helps you examine candidate moves, compare ideas, and understand critical moments.

AI Mentor

Optional guided explanations help translate analysis into understandable plans and strategic ideas.

Smart Library

Save and organize positions, games, and analysis for later review, with optional account features where available.

CAISSA Insight

Analyze your entire Chess.com game history to identify patterns, mistakes, and improvement areas.

Engine vs Engine

Watch AI battle AI. Test openings, explore positions, and learn from computer play.

Position Forge

Build custom positions with drag-and-drop editing and instant FEN generation.

How CAISSA Helps

CAISSA combines modern browser-based chess tools with guided study and practical explanations. The goal is to keep powerful analysis approachable while giving players clear ways to explore positions, review games, and build understanding.

  • Analyze – Examine games and positions with strong chess analysis.
  • Study – Learn through focused concepts, instructional positions, and guided practice.
  • Organize – Keep useful games and positions available for review.
  • Explore – Use opening, endgame, database, and classic-play experiences from one product.

Privacy & Data

Your privacy matters. CAISSA uses a mix of browser-based features and online services, depending on the experience you choose.

  • Browser Storage – Some preferences, saved work, and training progress can be kept in your browser.
  • Optional Accounts – Signing in can enable account and synchronization features where offered.
  • Online Requests – Features such as account services, payments, game imports, downloads, and optional AI assistance may contact online services.
  • Optional Analytics – CAISSA uses Microsoft Clarity on selected public pages to understand aggregate navigation, clicks, scrolling, device behavior, and usability issues. Interactive areas that may contain personal or chess data are masked. Clarity may process limited interaction and device data outside your browser even when analytics cookies are declined.
  • Your Choices – Features that send information online are initiated through the related product action.
