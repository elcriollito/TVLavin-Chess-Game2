    A Social Chess Lobby, Not Just a Pairing Button

    CAISSA Classic emphasizes the shared setting around a game. The room directory, visible tables, player activity, challenges, and spectator controls give players a sense of place before the first move. The CAISSA Lobby is the active public room, while the interface also presents dedicated spaces for tournaments, computer play, and teaching as clearly labelled parts of the Classic experience.

    What You Can Do in CAISSA Classic

    Browse tables and game information, create a casual or rated table, choose a time control and color preference, and follow activity from the lobby workspace. Board controls, an activity feed, rating legend, challenge actions, spectator options, room navigation, and Classic sound controls remain together in the application. Features that are not yet active are identified in the interface instead of being presented as available.

    Inspired by Online Chess History

    CAISSA preserves the social idea of room-based online chess while using its own identity, artwork, and implementation. It is not an official Yahoo product, clone, recreation, or continuation. Read the story behind CAISSA Classic, learn more about CAISSA Chess, or explore more chess writing on the CAISSA blog.

    About CAISSA Chess

    CAISSA Chess is a full-featured online chess application with multiple game modes and analysis tools.

    Features

    • Human vs Engine: Play against Stockfish with adjustable difficulty (20 levels)
    • Human vs Human: Play with a friend on the same device
    • Engine vs Engine: Watch two AI engines battle it out
    • Analysis Mode: Analyze positions with engine evaluation
    • Board Editor: Set up custom positions
    • Game Library: Study games from World Champions and Grandmasters
    • Move Navigation: Review games move-by-move

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How do I change engine difficulty?

    In the Play section, use "Engine Settings" panel on the right to adjust Stockfish strength from beginner to maximum.

    Can I import my Chess.com/Lichess games?

    Yes! Go to Analyze section, enter your username, and CAISSA will fetch your recent games for analysis.

    What is Caissa Insight?

    Caissa Insight analyzes your games and generates a personalized coaching report with strengths, weaknesses, and improvement tips.

    Is my data stored?

    Some CAISSA data can be stored in your browser. Imports, downloads, accounts, payments, and optional AI features may contact online services when you use them.

    How do I use Mentor AI?

    Click Mentor in the sidebar, send the current position, then ask questions about plans, moves, or concepts.
