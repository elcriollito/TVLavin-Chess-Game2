Questions

Reader FAQ

Can I buy CAISSA PGN Reader now?

No. Production Buy and public pricing remain disabled during prelaunch.

Where will entitled downloads appear?

In My Downloads after CAISSA verifies the account entitlement. The client never chooses a Blob pathname.

Does the Portable require development tools?

No. The independent RC1 clean gate ran without Node, npm, Cargo, Rust or Git installed.

Is RC1 digitally signed?

No. RC1 is the preserved unsigned baseline. Authenticode signing applies only to a later final candidate.

Are local PGN or FEN files uploaded?

The Reader is designed to keep private game content local; Market delivery does not receive PGN or FEN content.
