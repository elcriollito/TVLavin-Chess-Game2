CAISSA Chess legal draft

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the information CAISSA Chess processes when you use its chess tools, accounts, online play, Scanner, feedback and related services.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Review status: This is a pre-publication legal draft based on a technical audit. It is not yet approved for production publication.

1. Scope and who operates the service

CAISSA Chess is a free personal project and the public-facing name of the chess website and services available at www.caissa-chess.org. The service is individually operated by Alexander Lavin in Florida, United States. CAISSA Chess is not a separate legal entity, company, limited liability company or corporation.

CAISSA does not currently charge for services, operate commercial subscriptions or generate commercial revenue. Questions and privacy requests may be sent to privacy@caissa-chess.org. Please do not include passwords, access tokens or other secrets.

2. Information we process

The information depends on the features you choose. It can include:

  • account identifiers, email address, optional first and last name, profile image and authentication/session information;
  • a public chess nickname or system-generated player alias;
  • games, moves, positions, FEN or PGN data, ratings, puzzle attempts, saved library items and feature progress;
  • Scanner images submitted for recognition, image hashes, extracted positions, confidence data, corrections, capture type and limited client metadata;
  • feedback text and the consent choices attached to feedback;
  • feature-access, beta-program and entitlement records used to make free features available;
  • technical request information normally processed by hosting and security services, such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps and request logs; and
  • preferences and temporary working data stored in your browser.

3. Accounts and authentication

CAISSA uses Clerk for account registration, sign-in, session management and account security. Clerk may process your email address, account identifiers, optional profile fields, profile image, authentication factors, device/session information and security events. CAISSA stores a Clerk subject identifier and may store your email address in its application database to connect your account to saved features and entitlements.

The registration candidate uses short-lived abuse-control windows keyed by server-generated HMAC digests of request source and invitation recipient. It does not store the raw source address or email in those limiter records, and it does not combine those records with the submitted birth month or year.

Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is being prepared but was not active in CAISSA production at the date of this draft. If it is activated, CAISSA will request only the basic OpenID Connect identity scopes openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile. Google and Clerk will process the sign-in flow. CAISSA may receive the Google account subject identifier, email address and basic profile information made available by those scopes, use them to create or connect your CAISSA account and session, and share them with Clerk for that purpose. CAISSA will not receive your Google password, request Gmail, Drive or Calendar access, or use Google identity data for advertising.

4. Chess games, identity and activity

When you use account-connected features, CAISSA may store saved positions and collections, puzzle outcomes and rating progress, feature-access records, and activity needed to provide the service.

CAISSA Online records a public nickname or fallback alias, ratings and rating history, matchmaking or challenge state, games, moves, clocks, results and game events. Some public identity and chess information may be shown to other players. Email addresses and private account names are not intended to be copied into the public chess identity record.

5. CAISSA Scanner

When you choose a photograph or screenshot for Scanner recognition, the image is sent to CAISSA's recognition endpoint so the service can locate the board, classify squares and return a proposed chess position. CAISSA does not intentionally place the source image from an ordinary recognition request into its application database or Scanner evidence bucket. Hosting and security providers may still process the request and retain limited operational or security logs under their own schedules.

After recognition, CAISSA records a derived scan snapshot even if you do not submit feedback. That record can include an image hash, proposed FEN position, square predictions and confidence values, detected corners, board orientation, model and localization versions, capture type, viewport size and performance or diagnostic metadata. It is used to operate, secure and evaluate Scanner. You must review and edit the proposed position before relying on it.

Optional feedback and private image evidence

Submitting Scanner feedback is optional and requires an account. A feedback record can include the original and corrected positions, changed squares, model and localization versions, image hash, capture context and your separate consent choices. The underlying image is uploaded to a private Supabase Storage bucket only when you expressly choose to share it for improvement. The application checks account ownership and consent before accepting a reference to that image in feedback and keeps the storage reference private.

The current production upload and feedback-binding steps are not one atomic transaction. If the browser closes, the network fails or feedback submission is interrupted after an image upload, a private image object can remain without a completed feedback record. CAISSA has prepared stronger binding and cleanup controls, but they are not active in production. Historical private-beta Scanner images and records remain quarantined from training unless they separately satisfy the required consent and human-certification gates.

No automatic training: public Scanner feedback enters the system as raw-unverified. It is not automatically certified and is not automatically used to retrain or update the model. Training eligibility requires separate human review, the required consents and additional safeguards.

6. Why we process information

  • to authenticate users and protect accounts;
  • to provide chess tools, saved content, online games, ratings and progress;
  • to process Scanner images and return recognition results;
  • to receive optional feedback and conduct controlled, human-reviewed product improvement;
  • to manage free feature access, beta eligibility and account entitlements;
  • to prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, investigate failures and keep services secure; and
  • to understand aggregate site usage and maintain performance.

7. Service providers and external services

Verified service providers and integrations include:

  • Clerk for authentication and sessions;
  • Supabase for application database, realtime features and private Scanner feedback storage;
  • Vercel for website and serverless hosting, request processing and aggregate Web Analytics;
  • Cloudflare for authoritative DNS and selected gateway, download, puzzle or security services;
  • Chess.com, Lichess, FICS, ChessBase and other chess services when you use a feature that requests or embeds their content; and
  • Together AI, OpenAI, Anthropic or Meta's Llama API when you choose optional AI assistance. Relevant prompts, message history, chess or engine context and request settings are sent through CAISSA's server to the selected provider. If a user-supplied provider key is used, CAISSA forwards it to that provider for the request and does not intentionally store it in the application database or browser storage. The selected provider may retain request information under its own terms and privacy practices.

Microsoft Clarity integration code remains in the repository, but it is retired from current production documents and is not allowed by the active production content-security policy. It is not part of the current production data flow. This policy and the applicable consent controls must be updated before Clarity is reactivated.

No payment processor, paid subscription, renewal or refund program is active for CAISSA. Any future commercial feature will require a separate review and an updated Privacy Policy and Terms of Service before activation.

8. Cookies, analytics and browser storage

Clerk uses cookies or comparable browser mechanisms to maintain authentication sessions and protect sign-in flows. Vercel Web Analytics is configured on CAISSA production hosts to measure aggregate page visits; CAISSA removes query parameters and URL fragments before those analytics events are sent and blocks known credential-bearing contexts.

Microsoft Clarity is not loaded by the current production pages. A historical local analytics-consent value may remain in a browser that previously interacted with an earlier build, but the current site does not read that value to load Clarity.

CAISSA also uses localStorage and sessionStorage for interface language, onboarding state, navigation state, chess-engine and feature preferences, local profiles or wallet state in legacy experiences, puzzle synchronization queues, and temporary analysis or PGN handoffs. These items remain on your device until the application expires or replaces them, or you clear browser data.

9. Data retention

CAISSA keeps information for as long as it is reasonably needed to provide the relevant feature, protect the service, maintain transaction or audit integrity, comply with applicable obligations, or address a valid request. Some temporary sessions, invitations and challenges have code-defined expirations. Browser storage remains until the application replaces it or you clear it.

CAISSA does not currently operate one automated retention or deletion schedule covering every account, game and Scanner record. Account-linked chess data generally remains while the account or relevant feature remains active, subject to a verified request and records that must be preserved for security or integrity. Scanner audit and certification records are append-only by design, and uploaded feedback images do not yet have an automated expiration rule. Private historical Scanner evidence remains quarantined from training. Provider request logs, backups and deleted-data remnants may expire on provider-controlled schedules.

10. Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to create an account, submit Scanner feedback, share a Scanner image for improvement, share a correction for improvement, use external integrations or use optional AI assistance. You can clear CAISSA browser data through your browser settings.

You may ask to access, correct or delete information associated with you by contacting the address above. CAISSA will need to verify the request and will evaluate it subject to applicable law, security needs, technical constraints, provider capabilities and records that must be preserved for integrity. CAISSA does not currently provide a universal automated export or deletion feature and does not promise a response period shorter than applicable law requires. Deleting a Clerk identity alone may not remove all application records unless the related CAISSA request is also completed.

11. Security practices

For routes that require an account or elevated role, CAISSA uses authenticated server endpoints and access controls. Selected routes also use same-origin checks, rate limits, private storage, row-level security, restricted service credentials, content-security policies and human review gates for sensitive Scanner feedback. No system is perfectly secure, and CAISSA cannot guarantee that information will never be lost, misused or accessed without authorization.

12. International users

CAISSA uses service providers that operate in multiple countries. The production Supabase database, authentication data and Storage objects are primarily hosted in the United States in the East US (North Virginia) region. Supabase, Clerk, Vercel and their subprocessors may also process limited information in the United States and other countries for hosting, security, support, logs and content delivery.

Those providers publish data-processing terms and international-transfer mechanisms, including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable. CAISSA has not completed a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction transfer assessment, so this draft does not promise that information remains only in one country.

13. Children's privacy

CAISSA is a general-audience chess service and is not designed specifically for children. You must be at least 16 years old to create a new CAISSA account. If the law where you live requires a higher minimum age or additional permission to use an online service, that requirement also applies.

New accounts for people under 16 are not currently offered. A future CAISSA Junior initiative may support younger users only after a separate legal and product review, verifiable parental consent where required, and the privacy and social safeguards required by applicable law.

Some account-free chess content and tools may remain publicly accessible where appropriate. Public access does not authorize a child to create an account or submit personal information. A data-minimizing technical candidate asks only for birth month and year, evaluates them transiently before email or OAuth, and does not retain those values. It is not active in production; new account registration remains paused pending the Human Release Gate. A parent or guardian may contact CAISSA about a child's information.

14. Changes to this policy

CAISSA may update this policy as its services, providers and legal obligations change. The page will show a revised "Last updated" date. For a material change, CAISSA proposes to provide reasonable additional notice through the service or account email, and to request renewed consent where applicable law requires it. The current site does not yet automate that notice or re-consent process, so the final procedure requires operator and qualified legal approval before publication.

15. Contact

The legal operator is Alexander Lavin, an individual located in Florida, United States. For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@caissa-chess.org. CAISSA has not published a residential, company or business address. Whether a separate postal notice address or representative is required for any applicable jurisdiction remains under qualified legal review.
